# RESERVE BANK OF INDIA: GUIDELINES ON DIGITAL LENDING
**Circular No: RBI/2022-23/111 DOR.CRE.REC.65/21.01.003/2022-23**
*Department of Regulation, Reserve Bank of India, Central Office, Mumbai*

---

## 1. APPLICABILITY AND CLASSIFICATION

### 1.1 Scope of Application
These guidelines apply to all Digital Lending transactions extended by:
- (a) All Commercial Banks (including Small Finance Banks, Payments Banks, Regional Rural Banks);
- (b) Primary (Urban) Co-operative Banks, State Co-operative Banks, and Central Co-operative Banks; and
- (c) Non-Banking Financial Companies (NBFCs) including Housing Finance Companies (HFCs).
The above entities are collectively referred to as **Regulated Entities (REs)**.

### 1.2 Key Definitions
- **Digital Lending**: A remote and automated lending process, largely by use of seamless digital technologies for customer acquisition, credit assessment, loan approval, disbursement, recovery, and associated customer service.
- **Digital Lending Apps / Platforms (DLAs)**: Mobile and web-based applications with user interfaces through which a borrower can apply for a digital loan, operated directly by the RE or by a Lending Service Provider (LSP) engaged by the RE.
- **Lending Service Provider (LSP)**: An agent of a Regulated Entity who carries out one or more of lender's functions or services in customer acquisition, underwriting support, pricing support, servicing, monitoring, and recovery of loans of RE on digital lending platforms.
- **Annual Percentage Rate (APR)**: The effective annualized rate charged to the borrower of a digital loan, including the base interest rate and all associated fees, processing charges, administrative costs, and statutory taxes.
- **Key Fact Statement (KFS)**: A standardized, simple, one-page summary document provided to the borrower before loan execution detailing loan terms, APR, recovery mechanisms, and grievance officer contacts.

## 2. CUSTOMER PROTECTION AND CONDUCT REQUIREMENTS

### 2.1 Loan Disbursal, Servicing, and Repayment
- (a) **Direct Bank Account Disbursal**: All loan disbursements must be executed directly into the verified bank account of the borrower, without passing through any pool account or third-party account of the Lending Service Provider (LSP) or any other intermediary.
- (b) **Direct Repayment**: All loan repayments must be made directly from the borrower's bank account to the RE's bank account without any pass-through or escrow account maintained by any third party or LSP.
- (c) **Exception for Co-lending / Merchant Transactions**: In case of co-lending arrangements or point-of-sale merchant financing, disbursements may directly credit the seller/merchant account, provided explicit documentation and end-use verification exists.
- (d) **Fee Transparency**: Under no circumstance shall any fee, charge, or commission payable to an LSP be collected directly from the borrower by the LSP. Any fee payable to the LSP must be paid directly by the RE.

### 2.2 Key Fact Statement (KFS) and Disclosures
- (a) REs must provide a standardized KFS to the borrower in a format prescribed by RBI before execution of the loan contract.
- (b) The KFS must explicitly disclose:
  - (i) Loan amount, tenor, and repayment schedule;
  - (ii) Annual Percentage Rate (APR) computed using the internal rate of return formula;
  - (iii) Breakdown of all upfront fees, processing charges, and penal charges;
  - (iv) Cooling-off / Look-up period details;
  - (v) Details of the designated Nodal Grievance Redressal Officer.
- (c) No fees, charges, or terms not explicitly disclosed in the KFS shall be charged to the borrower at any stage of the loan lifecycle.

### 2.3 Cooling-off / Look-up Period
- (a) A mandatory cooling-off / look-up period must be provided to the borrower during which the borrower can exit the digital loan without penalty:
  - (i) For loans having a tenor of **seven days or more**, the cooling-off period shall not be less than **three (3) days**;
  - (ii) For loans having a tenor of **less than seven days**, the cooling-off period shall not be less than **one (1) day**.
- (b) To exercise the exit option, the borrower must repay the principal amount along with the proportionate APR accrued for the elapsed period without any prepayment or foreclosure penalties.

### 2.4 Automatic Credit Limit Increase
- (a) Automatic increase in credit limit without explicit, recorded consent of the borrower is strictly prohibited.
- (b) Any enhancement of credit limit must follow a fresh credit assessment and obtain verifiable consent from the borrower.

## 3. TECHNOLOGY AND DATA PRIVACY REQUIREMENTS

### 3.1 Collection and Use of Borrower Data
- (a) **Need-Based Collection**: Collection of personal data by DLAs and LSPs must be strictly need-based, with clear economic and operational justification, and preceded by explicit prior consent of the borrower.
- (b) **Prohibited Access to Device Resources**: DLAs and LSPs are strictly prohibited from accessing mobile device resources such as:
  - (i) Contact lists / address books;
  - (ii) Call logs and telephone logs;
  - (iii) SMS message contents;
  - (iv) File storage, photos, videos, and media files;
  - (v) Biometric data of any kind.
- (c) **Permitted One-Time Access**: One-time access to camera, microphone, and location may be requested solely for the purpose of onboarding and Video Customer Identification Process (V-CIP) / KYC, subject to explicit consent.
- (d) **Right to Revoke & Erase**: Borrowers must be provided the option to revoke consent for data sharing and demand erasure/deletion of data collected by the DLA or LSP.

### 3.2 Data Storage and Localization
- (a) **Physical Localization in India**: All data collected by REs, DLAs, and LSPs pertaining to digital lending must be stored exclusively in servers physically located within the territorial boundaries of India.
- (b) **LSP Data Retention Restrictions**: LSPs are prohibited from storing personal information of borrowers except for basic minimal data such as name, address, contact details, and transaction reference numbers necessary for operational verification.
- (c) **Biometric Data Storage Ban**: No biometric data of borrowers shall be stored on any systems or servers of the RE, DLA, or LSP under any circumstances.

## 4. CODE OF CONDUCT FOR RECOVERY AND GRIEVANCE REDRESSAL

### 4.1 Recovery Practices and Code of Conduct
- (a) The RE shall be directly responsible for the actions of its LSPs and debt recovery agents.
- (b) Recovery agents are strictly prohibited from:
  - (i) Harassing or abusing borrowers verbally or physically;
  - (ii) Contacting borrowers before 08:00 AM or after 07:00 PM;
  - (iii) Contacting persons in borrower's contact lists who are not co-borrowers or guarantors;
  - (iv) Sending humiliating, threatening, or obscene messages on social media or messaging platforms.
- (c) The name of the recovery agent assigned to an account must be communicated to the borrower via SMS/email prior to any contact.

### 4.2 Grievance Redressal Mechanism
- (a) REs and LSPs must designate a dedicated **Nodal Grievance Redressal Officer** whose name, designation, email address, physical address, and telephone number are prominently displayed on the website and DLA interface.
- (b) Complaints lodged by the borrower must be resolved within a maximum statutory period of **thirty (30) days**.
- (c) If a complaint is not resolved within 30 days or if the borrower is dissatisfied with the resolution, the borrower has the right to escalate the grievance to the **Reserve Bank - Integrated Ombudsman Scheme (RB-IOS)**.
