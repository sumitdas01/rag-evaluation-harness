# SECURITIES AND EXCHANGE BOARD OF INDIA: BRSR FRAMEWORK
**Master Circular: SEBI/HO/CFD/CFD-SEC-2/P/CIR/2023/122**
*Securities and Exchange Board of India (Corporation Finance Department), Mumbai*

---

## 1. REGULATORY MANDATE AND STRUCTURE

### 1.1 Scope and Applicability
- (a) Business Responsibility and Sustainability Reporting (BRSR) is mandatory for the **top 1,000 listed entities** by market capitalization on the Bombay Stock Exchange (BSE) and National Stock Exchange of India (NSE).
- (b) The BRSR report must be integrated as an essential component of the Annual Report of the listed company filed with SEBI and stock exchanges.
- (c) **BRSR Core**: A sub-set of key performance indicators (KPIs) focusing on measurable metrics such as GHG footprints, water intensity, gender diversity, and fair wages for which mandatory independent reasonable assurance is prescribed.

### 1.2 Structure of BRSR Report
The reporting format consists of three distinct sections:
- **Section A: General Disclosures**: Details of the listed entity, products/services, operations, employees (including gender ratios and employee turnover), holding/subsidiary companies, and CSR activities.
- **Section B: Management and Process Disclosures**: Governance structures, sustainability policies, board oversight, risk management frameworks, and grievance redressal mechanisms for ESG issues.
- **Section C: Principle-wise Performance Disclosures**: Quantitative and qualitative disclosures mapped across the **Nine Principles of the National Guidelines on Responsible Business Conduct (NGRBC)**.

## 2. KEY PRINCIPLE-WISE PERFORMANCE REQUIREMENTS

### 2.1 Principle 1: Integrity, Ethics, and Transparency
- (a) **Anti-Bribery and Corruption**: The company must disclose details of its anti-corruption policy, coverage across operations and supply chain partners, and training provided to directors, employees, and value chain partners.
- (b) **Whistleblower Mechanism**: Disclosures on the number of complaints received regarding ethical breaches, bribery, and conflicts of interest, along with the percentage of complaints resolved during the financial year.
- (c) **Regulatory Fines and Sanctions**: Disclosure of monetary fines, penalties, settlements, and non-monetary enforcement actions imposed by regulators (such as SEBI, RBI, CCI, or MCA) exceeding ₹1 lakh.

### 2.2 Principle 3: Employee Well-being and Safety
- (a) **Social Protection & Insurance**: Mandatory disclosure of percentage of permanent and non-permanent employees covered by health insurance, accident insurance, and maternity/paternity benefits. Listed entities must aim for 100% coverage.
- (b) **Gender Diversity and Pay Parity**:
  - (i) Ratio of female employees to total workforce across managerial and board levels;
  - (ii) Ratio of median remuneration of female employees to male employees in equivalent designations;
  - (iii) Details of Internal Complaints Committee (ICC) established under POSH Act (Prevention of Sexual Harassment) and number of complaints filed and resolved.
- (c) **Occupational Health and Safety**:
  - (i) Lost Time Injury Frequency Rate (LTIFR);
  - (ii) Number of workplace fatalities and high-consequence work-related injuries, with a target of zero harm.

### 2.3 Principle 6: Environmental Stewardship and Climate Action
- (a) **Greenhouse Gas (GHG) Emissions**:
  - (i) **Scope 1 Emissions**: Direct emissions from company-owned or controlled sources (metric tonnes of CO2 equivalent);
  - (ii) **Scope 2 Emissions**: Indirect emissions from the generation of purchased electricity, steam, heating, and cooling;
  - (iii) **Scope 3 Emissions**: Optional for general BRSR, but mandatory for BRSR Core value chain disclosures;
  - (iv) GHG intensity metric calculated per rupee of turnover or unit of production.
- (b) **Water Security**: Total water withdrawal by source (groundwater, municipal, surface water, recycled water), water consumption intensity per unit of revenue, and zero-liquid-discharge (ZLD) implementation status.
- (c) **Waste Management and Circular Economy**: Quantification of total plastic waste, e-waste, hazardous waste generated, recycled, re-used, and safely disposed through co-processing or authorized recyclers.

### 2.4 Principle 8: Inclusive Growth and Corporate Social Responsibility (CSR)
- (a) **Statutory CSR Expenditure**: Compliance with Section 135 of the Companies Act, 2013, requiring companies meeting eligibility thresholds (net worth ₹500 crore, turnover ₹1,000 crore, or net profit ₹5 crore) to spend at least **two percent (2%)** of average net profits of preceding three financial years on approved CSR programs.
- (b) **Social Impact Assessments (SIA)**: Mandatory independent impact assessments for CSR projects with outlays exceeding ₹1 crore, completed through accredited external agencies.
- (c) **Local Sourcing and MSME Procurement**: Percentage of total procurement value sourced directly from Micro, Small, and Medium Enterprises (MSMEs) and local district vendors within 50 km of operational facilities.

## 3. ASSURANCE AND VALUE CHAIN COMPLIANCE

### 3.1 BRSR Core and Mandatory Reasonable Assurance
- (a) Beginning from FY 2023-24 for the top 150 listed entities (and extended sequentially to the top 1,000 listed entities), companies must obtain **independent reasonable assurance** on their BRSR Core disclosures.
- (b) The assurance provider must be an independent entity possessing verifiable technical competence in ESG auditing and cannot have any conflict of interest with the financial auditor of the listed company.

### 3.2 Value Chain Disclosures
- (a) Disclosures for BRSR Core must extend to the **value chain** of the listed company, encompassing top upstream suppliers and downstream customers comprising at least **75% of purchases or sales** by value.
- (b) Listed companies must actively audit and report ESG KPIs of key value chain partners regarding child labor, minimum wages, and environmental compliance.
